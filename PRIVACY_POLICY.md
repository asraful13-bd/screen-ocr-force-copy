# Privacy Policy for Screen OCR & Force Copy

**Last updated:** November 2026

Screen OCR & Force Copy is developed by Md. Asraful Alam. We value your privacy.

### Data Collection and Usage
- We **do not collect, store, sell, or share** any personally identifiable information.
- Screen capture is processed in real-time solely upon user interaction (via keyboard shortcut or popup button).
- The cropped image is transmitted securely to the OCR API exclusively for extracting text and is not stored permanently.
- The extracted text is saved directly to your local system clipboard.
